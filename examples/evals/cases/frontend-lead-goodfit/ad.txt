Senior Frontend Engineer (Team Lead) — Meadowlark (Manchester, hybrid)

About the role
We're a 40-person product startup building developer tooling. We're looking for
a Senior Frontend Engineer to lead a small team of 4 engineers. This is a
player-coach role: roughly half your time hands-on in our React + TypeScript
codebase, half growing and supporting the team.

You'll
- Lead feature delivery across our React/TypeScript web app and its Node.js API.
- Mentor engineers, run code reviews, and shape our frontend architecture.
- Work closely with product and design on a shared roadmap.

We're looking for
- Several years of senior frontend / full-stack experience with React and
  TypeScript.
- Some experience leading or mentoring engineers (formal management optional).
- Comfort across a Node.js backend.

Location: Manchester, hybrid (2 days a week in our city-centre office).
