## My three tracks

- **Track A — manager:** Engineering Manager or Head of Engineering of a small
  software team (roughly 3–8 engineers). Player-coach roles especially welcome.
- **Track B — lead_ic:** hands-on Senior/Lead/Staff engineer in frontend,
  full-stack or product engineering on React/TypeScript/Node. Tech Lead and
  Team Lead roles count.
- **Track C — tpm (a realistic stretch):** Technical Program Manager or
  Engineering Program Manager in a software organisation, at a level I could
  realistically reach — the role accepts engineering leadership as relevant, or
  asks for roughly ≤5 years of program management. Not Staff/Principal TPM, and
  not non-technical project management.

## How to score (1–5)

- **5:** strong fit on any track; location works; seniority matches.
- **4:** good fit with one manageable gap.
- **3:** a possible stretch; worth a look.
- **1–2:** junior/mid IC, director-level at a big org, outside my location
  rules, a backend-only role in a language I don't use (Go, Java, C#, Python),
  non-software engineering, or core requirements I clearly don't meet.

Always state the main reason it fits and the biggest gap, honestly.
